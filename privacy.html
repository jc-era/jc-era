Privacy Policy

Formal data protection standards for our global mobile applications and services.

Last Updated: October 2023

1. Data Collection & Usage

Beijing Jingcheng Shidai Technology Co., Ltd. ("we", "us") collects specific information to provide, maintain, and improve our services. This includes:

  • Device Information: Device model, OS version, unique device identifiers (IDFA/GAID), and network information.
  • Usage Data: Application interaction logs, performance metrics, and in-game progress.
  • Transaction Data: Purchase history for in-app items and subscriptions (processed securely via Apple/Google).

2. Age Policy & Child Protection

We take children's privacy seriously and strictly adhere to the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and GDPR-K:

  • Age Rating: Our applications are rated according to global standards (ESRB, PEGI, IARC). Users must meet the minimum age requirement specified in the application's store listing.
  • Minors' Data: We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the legal age in your jurisdiction). If we discover such data has been collected, we will take immediate steps to delete it.
  • Parental Rights: Parents can request the review or deletion of their child's data by contacting support@jc-era.com.

3. Global Compliance Standards

Our services are designed to be compliant with the highest global privacy standards:

  • GDPR (EEA): We provide legal bases for processing and respect your rights to access, rectification, and erasure.
  • CCPA/CPRA (California): We disclose our data collection categories and provide "Do Not Sell My Personal Information" options where applicable.
  • Google Play & App Store: All data collection practices are disclosed in the Data Safety section and App Privacy labels of the respective stores.

4. Monetization & Third-Party Disclosure

To support our free services, we utilize professional monetization platforms. These third parties may access pseudonymous data for advertising and analytics:

  • Ad Networks: AdMob, Unity Ads, and Facebook Audience Network.
  • Analytics: Firebase and Google Analytics.

We ensure all partners maintain high security standards and comply with international data transfer regulations (e.g., Standard Contractual Clauses).

5. Data Security & Retention

We implement robust technical and organizational measures to protect your data. Information is retained only as long as necessary to provide services or comply with legal obligations.

6. Contact & Redress

For any privacy inquiries or to exercise your data rights, please contact our Data Protection Office:
Email: support@jc-era.com
